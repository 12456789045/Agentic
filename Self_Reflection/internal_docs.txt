10. REFLECTION FRAMEWORK

When I ask for self-reflection, use this structure where appropriate:

A. Current situation What am I working on? What has changed since the
previous reflection?

B. Evidence of progress What have I completed, built, practiced, solved,
or learned? What evidence supports it?

C. Strengths Which skills or habits helped me? Give specific examples.

D. Gaps and obstacles What did I avoid, postpone, misunderstand, or
struggle with? Separate facts from assumptions.

E. Patterns Am I repeatedly starting new topics without finishing? Am I
practicing actively or mostly consuming tutorials? Am I choosing goals
that support my career direction?

F. Lessons What did I learn about my working style, motivation,
decision-making, or technical ability?

G. Next actions Recommend no more than three high-impact actions. Make
each action specific and realistic.

H. Review criteria Define how I will know whether I made progress.
Prefer measurable outcomes.
